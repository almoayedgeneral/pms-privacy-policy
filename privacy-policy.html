Privacy Policy

Almoayed Homes Property Management System (PMS)

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Almoayed Homes (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in connection with the Almoayed Homes Property Management System (PMS) and our business communications through WhatsApp.

1. Information we may process

Depending on how a tenant, resident or other user communicates with us, we may process:

  • WhatsApp phone number, profile information made available through WhatsApp, message content, timestamps and message identifiers;
  • information provided about a property, apartment, maintenance issue, facility reservation or service enquiry;
  • relevant property-management correspondence and request-status information; and
  • technical event and message-delivery information received through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API).

Please do not send payment-card details, passwords or other highly sensitive information through WhatsApp.

2. How we use information

We may use information to identify and communicate with tenants and residents; receive, register and respond to maintenance requests and facility-reservation enquiries; provide acknowledgements and request-status updates; coordinate property-management services; maintain operational records; troubleshoot and secure the service; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp and Meta

Our PMS uses an integration with the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). When you message our business WhatsApp number, relevant message content and technical messaging data may be processed by Meta and transmitted to the application endpoint configured by Almoayed Homes so that your enquiry can be handled. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's applicable terms and privacy information.

Almoayed Homes business WhatsApp number: +973 1755 6666.

4. Sharing and service providers

We may make relevant information available to authorized Almoayed Homes personnel who need it to handle property-management matters. Information may also be processed by service providers supporting application hosting, operation, security or messaging, where applicable and necessary. We may disclose information when required by law or a valid legal process.

5. Storage, security and retention

Information may be stored in the Almoayed Homes property-management systems and in systems used to provide the messaging service. We apply safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure, and access should be limited to authorized personnel and service providers who need it for their duties.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the property-management purpose for which it was collected, operational requirements and applicable legal obligations. Information may then be deleted or anonymized where appropriate. Specific retention periods may depend on the type of record and applicable requirements.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Access, correction and deletion requests

You may ask Almoayed Homes about personal information associated with your property-management communications, or request correction or deletion where applicable. To submit a request, contact our business WhatsApp number at +973 1755 6666 and state that your message is a privacy or personal-data request. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information in the request.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and relationship to the relevant record before acting. We will assess and respond to requests in accordance with applicable requirements. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate property-management recordkeeping.

7. Children

The PMS and its WhatsApp support channel are intended for property-management communications by tenants, residents and other relevant adults. They are not designed to solicit personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates when this version was issued. The latest version will be made available at this page.
