1. Information we may process

Depending on how a tenant or user communicates with us, we may process:

  • WhatsApp phone number, message content, timestamps, and message identifiers;
  • information a tenant provides about a property, maintenance issue, or service enquiry;
  • relevant property-management correspondence and request-status information; and
  • technical event and delivery-status information received through the WhatsApp Cloud API.

We do not intend to request information unnecessary for property-management communications. Please do not send payment-card details, passwords, or other highly sensitive information through WhatsApp.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to receive and respond to tenant enquiries, coordinate maintenance and property services, provide relevant acknowledgements or updates, maintain operational records, troubleshoot message delivery, protect the service, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp and Meta

Our PMS is designed to integrate with the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). When people communicate with our WhatsApp business number, relevant message content and technical messaging data may be processed by Meta and transmitted to our configured application endpoint. Meta also processes information under its own terms and privacy policies.

Our business WhatsApp number is +973 1755 6666. Messaging features may be unavailable or delayed while technical issues are investigated.

4. Sharing and service providers

We may make relevant information available to authorized property-management personnel and to service providers that support hosting, application operation, security, or messaging, where applicable and necessary. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law. Our organization should ensure this description matches the providers actually configured.

5. Storage, security, and retention

We use appropriate safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Access should be limited to authorized personnel and service providers who need it for their duties. Information is retained only as long as needed for the relevant property-management purpose, operational requirements, or legal obligations, and then deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Requests about your information

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your property-management communications or request correction or deletion where applicable. 7. Children

This service is intended for property-management communications by tenants, residents, and other relevant adults. It is not designed to solicit personal information from children.
